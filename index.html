One Year In: The Story of Us

By Tonton

It was dark that night, the kind of dark where you're not really looking for anything, just standing outside, letting the evening pass. Then she smiled. Just once. And somehow the whole place felt lit up, like someone had turned the lights on without anyone noticing.

I did not know it then the way I know it now, but some part of me already did. That was the night I met Passion, and it was the night I stopped standing outside in the dark the same way ever again.

"It was dark outside, but her smile lit up the whole place."

A year later, I still have not stopped thinking about her. Not for one single day. What started as one smile in the dark became the story of us, and this is only the front page.

The Girl Who Changed Everything

There was once a young man named Tonse, not broken, exactly, but missing something he couldn't quite name. He got by fine on his own. Something in him was just waiting.

Then came Passion, whose smile lit up his days and whose kindness made him want to be a better man. Her hugs felt like home before he even knew that was what he was looking for. Somewhere between her laugh and her patience, the missing piece found its place. She was the most beautiful girl he'd ever seen, and somehow, she chose him. It's her over anyone, and forever.

Chapter Two

She Works Hard For Everything

Passion is currently in her third year of biomedical engineering, and anyone who knows her knows exactly how much she puts into it. Late nights, early mornings, whatever it takes, she shows up for it every single time.

It's that kind of dedication, showing up even when it's hard, that makes her someone worth being proud of every single day.
